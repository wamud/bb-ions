These are IBM circuits that follow the interleaved syndrome extraction circuits of the OG BB paper (https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07107-7) and can be found at https://github.com/FranciscoRevson/relay-BP-IBM/tree/829f10931d7949c5f559e3a5e736ec0a71b4beb9/tests/testdata/bicycle_bivariate

I put selected four codes (mem X, p=0.001) of the relayBP circuits and put them in the directory "uniform noise", renamed them according to my circuit-naming convention with the addition of an SE (syndrome extraction) value. The ones from relayBP have "SE='OG_BB'".Than
